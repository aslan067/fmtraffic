Parking Barriers, Bollards & Wheel Stops

Parking barriers reserve private parking spaces: unlock and fold them down to park, raise and lock them when you leave. Steel bollards keep vehicles away from entrances, shop fronts and pedestrian areas. Wheel stops show drivers where to stop in each bay and protect walls and kerbs.
