Delivery terms

We deliver EXW (Ex Works) Türkiye: your freight forwarder collects the goods and handles transport to your site. Lead times depend on the products and quantities ordered; we confirm them with every quote.

EU–Türkiye Customs Union

Under the EU–Türkiye Customs Union, most industrial goods can be imported into the EU without customs duty when accompanied by an A.TR movement certificate. Import VAT and local rules still apply; your forwarder or customs broker can advise on your shipment.

CE certification

Our range is CE-certified. CE documentation is available on request for the products you order.

OEM / private label

We can supply products in your color, with your logo and in your packaging. Tell us your requirements with your enquiry.
